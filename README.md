# SOLMARA — City of Open Roads

An original offline Three.js crime game set in a sunlit coastal city. Driver-for-hire Marco Reyes returns home, rebuilds his crew, recovers a stolen ledger, and makes one final run for freedom.

Run `node serve.ts` and open `http://localhost:8080`. No install, build, or network access is needed to play. Three.js is vendored locally; scenery, characters, vehicles, textures, particles, and synthesized audio are generated by the game.

**Controls:** Enter starts; WASD or arrows move and drive; Shift runs; mouse looks after clicking the game; E takes or exits a car; Space handbrakes; left click fires; R reloads; F strikes; Escape pauses. Follow the gold beacon and minimap marker. Break police sightlines behind city blocks to lose heat. Staying boxed in leads to arrest.

Five linked missions feature a delivery, two associate pickups, an armed exchange, a wanted escape, and a timed finale. Completed jobs, wallet, and best survival score persist in localStorage. Pause offers a story reset; retry keeps completed jobs.

Focused source modules live under `game/src/`; the minified Three.js engine is under `game/vendor/`. `node tests/systems.mjs` checks mission progression and failure. `shots.json` defines four deterministic live simulation presets. With Playwright installed, run `node tools/capture.mjs` while serving; `PLAYWRIGHT_PATH` may point to a local installation. `node main.ts` builds the game and screenshot dossier in `out/` when `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out`.

The screenshots are uncropped browser renders at 1280×720; their staged states include city exploration, pursuit, mission targets, traffic, and pedestrians.
